history_edu 44+ Years of Educational Legacy

A Legacy of Christian Moral Values & Scholastic Renown

St. Isabel's Girls Higher Secondary School was established in 1981 at Matha Kovil Street, Pattukkottai, with the profound mission of providing accessible, high-standard higher secondary education to young women from all walks of life.

Under the benevolent guidance of the Congregation of the Sisters of Bon Secours, the institution blends uncompromising academic rigor with holistic virtues — discipline, charity, self-reliance, and spiritual uprightness. With over 2,700 students and 100 faculty members, St. Isabel's is recognized as a cornerstone institutional pillar of Thanjavur district.
